1. Fundamental Astronomical Engine & Coordinates (Pages 1 - 4)

1.1 Time System & Local Mean Time (LMT) Correction

All planetary positions are computed at the exact Universal Time (UT) corresponding to the native's local birth event. Indian Standard Time (IST) is synchronized with the reference longitude $82^\circ 30' ext{ E} = 82.5^\circ ext{ E}$.

Desantara (LMT Correction) in minutes = (Longitude_Place° - 82.5°) × 4.0
LMT = Standard_Time + Desantara
UT = Standard_Time - 5h 30m = LMT - (Longitude_Place° / 15.0)

Worked Example from Salem ($78^\circ 12' ext{ E}$):

ΔLongitude = 78.20° - 82.50° = -4.30° = -4° 18'
Desantara = -4.30 × 4 = -17.20 minutes = -17 minutes 12 seconds (-0:17:12).

1.2 Chitra Paksha (Lahiri) Ayanamsha Formulation

Nirayana (sidereal) longitudes are derived by deducting the true precession arc (Ayanamsha) from the Sayana (tropical) positions computed via high-precision planetary theory (VSOP87 / JPL DE405):

T = (Julian_Date_TT - 2451545.0) / 36525.0
Ayanamsha_Lahiri(T) = 23° 51' 25.53'' + 5029.0966'' × T + 1.1116'' × T² - 0.0001'' × T³ - Nutation_in_Longitude × cos(True_Obliquity)

1.3 Sunrise (`சூரிய உதயம்`) & Sunset (`சூரிய அஸ்தமனம்`) for Disc Center (`பிம்ப மத்தி`)

Unlike civil sunrise (which includes atmospheric refraction of 34' plus semi-diameter 16' = 50' below horizon), this system computes the rising/setting of the center of the solar disc (`பிம்ப மத்தி`):

True Zenith Distance z₀ = 90° + Atmospheric_Refraction(0.5667°)
cos(H₀) = [cos(z₀) - sin(Latitude) × sin(Sun_Declination)] / [cos(Latitude) × cos(Sun_Declination)]
Half_Day_Arc (Dinamana) = 2 × H₀ (converted to hours)
Sunrise_LMT = Local_Apparent_Noon - H₀
Sunset_LMT = Local_Apparent_Noon + H₀

1.4 Udayadi Nazhigai (`உதயாதி நாழிகை`)

The elapsed time from local sunrise to the moment of birth, measured in the ancient sexagesimal units of Ghati (Nazhigai) and Vighati (Vinazhigai):

If Birth_Time >= Sunrise: Elapsed_Hours = Birth_Time - Sunrise
If Birth_Time < Sunrise: Elapsed_Hours = (Birth_Time + 24.0) - Previous_Day_Sunrise
Total_Nazhigai = Elapsed_Hours / 0.4 (Since 1 Nazhigai = 24 minutes = 0.4 hours)
Nazhigai = floor(Total_Nazhigai)
Vinazhigai = floor((Total_Nazhigai - Nazhigai) × 60)
Tatpara = round((((Total_Nazhigai - Nazhigai) × 60) - Vinazhigai) × 60)

1.5 Janma Nakshatra, Pada & Mathematical Dasha Balance

Pada_Span = 3° 20' = 3.333333° = 200 arcminutes
Nakshatra_Index = floor(λ_Moon / 13.333333°)

Elapsed_Fraction (Garbhasel) = Arc_Within_Star / 13.333333°

2. Sripati Bhava Sphutam & House Shift Mechanics (Page 7)

2.1 Mathematical Construction of the Sripati Quadrants

Unlike Equal or Placidus houses, traditional South Indian Thirukanitha horoscopes uniformly apply Sripati Bhava Paddhati (derived from the classical text Sripati Paddhati):

1. Ascendant (Lagna) = Center of Bhava 1 (B₁) = RAMC & Obliquity solution at Horizon.
2. Medium Coeli (MC / Dasama Sphutam) = Center of Bhava 10 (B₁₀) = arctan[tan(RAMC) / cos(ε)].
3. Descendant = Center of Bhava 7 (B₇) = (B₁ + 180°) mod 360°.
4. Imum Coeli (IC) = Center of Bhava 4 (B₄) = (B₁₀ + 180°) mod 360°.

Quadrants are trisected to find the intermediate Bhava Madhyas (Centers):
Arc_Asc_to_IC = (B₄ - B₁) mod 360°
B₂ = (B₁ + Arc_Asc_to_IC / 3.0) mod 360°
B₃ = (B₁ + 2.0 × Arc_Asc_to_IC / 3.0) mod 360°

Arc_IC_to_Desc = (B₇ - B₄) mod 360°
B₅ = (B₄ + Arc_IC_to_Desc / 3.0) mod 360°
B₆ = (B₄ + 2.0 × Arc_IC_to_Desc / 3.0) mod 360°

B₈ = (B₂ + 180°) mod 360°; B₉ = (B₃ + 180°) mod 360°
B₁₁ = (B₅ + 180°) mod 360°; B₁₂ = (B₆ + 180°) mod 360°

2.2 Bhava Sandhi (Junctions: Arambam & Mudivu)

The boundaries of each Bhava are calculated as the arithmetic midpoints between consecutive centers:

Arambam(Bhava n) = (B_{n-1} + B_n) / 2.0
Mudivu(Bhava n) = (B_n + B_{n+1}) / 2.0
Note: Arambam of Bhava n is strictly equal to the Mudivu of Bhava (n-1).

2.3 Bhava Shift Detection Algorithm (`ஜாதகத்தில் பாவக மாற்றம்`)

Rashi_House = floor((λ_Planet - λ_Lagna) / 30°) + 1

4. Complete Shadbala (6-Fold Strength) Formulation (Page 13)

Shadbala measures the absolute physical, celestial, and temporal strength of the 7 planets (Sun to Saturn) in Virupas (60 Virupas = 1 Rupa). All values in Page 13 are printed in Rupas ($= ext{Virupas} / 60$).

4.1 Sthana Bala (Positional Strength - Max ~390 Virupas)

Sum of five sub-components:

Sthana_Bala = Uccha_Bala + Saptavargaja_Bala + Ojayugmarasyamsa_Bala + Kendradi_Bala + Drekkana_Bala
- Uccha_Bala: Degrees from deep debilitation point (Neecha) / 3.0 (Max = 60 Virupas).
- Saptavargaja_Bala: Sum of dignities across D1, D2, D3, D7, D9, D12, D30 (Moolatrikona: 45, Swakshetra: 30, Mitra: 15, Sama: 7.5, Satru: 3.75).
- Kendradi_Bala: In Kendra (1, 4, 7, 10) = 60 Virupas; Panaphara (2, 5, 8, 11) = 30; Apoklima (3, 6, 9, 12) = 15.
- Ojayugmarasyamsa_Bala: 15 Virupas if male planet in odd sign/navamsha, or female planet in even sign/navamsha.
- Drekkana_Bala: 15 Virupas according to male/hermaphrodite/female planetary lordship over 1st/2nd/3rd decanate.

4.2 Dik Bala (Directional Strength - Max 60 Virupas)

Zero-point (Nirbala): Jupiter & Mercury = 7th house (Descendant); Sun & Mars = 4th house (Nadir);
Saturn = 1st house (Ascendant); Moon & Venus = 10th house (Zenith).
Dik_Bala = |λ_Planet - Zero_Point_Degree| / 3.0 (if > 180°, subtract from 360°). Max = 60 Virupas.

4.3 Kala Bala (Temporal Strength - Max ~300 Virupas)

Kala_Bala = Natonnata_Bala + Paksha_Bala + Tribhaga_Bala + Varsha-Masa-Dina-Hora_Bala + Ayana_Bala + Yuddha_Bala
- Natonnata: Moon, Mars, Saturn strong at midnight (60); Sun, Jupiter, Venus strong at noon (60); Mercury always 60.
- Paksha_Bala: Benefics gain strength in Shukla Paksha; Malefics gain strength in Krishna Paksha (Max = 60).
- Ayana_Bala: Function of planetary declination (Kranti). Sun, Mars, Jupiter, Venus gain strength with North declination; Moon, Saturn with South declination.

4.4 Cheshta Bala (Motional Strength - Max 60 Virupas)

Computed from planetary speed relative to mean motion:
Retrograde (Vakra) = 60 Virupas; Stationary (Anuvakra) = 30 Virupas; Fast (Vikala) = 45 Virupas;
Slow (Manda) = 15 Virupas. (For Sun and Moon, Cheshta Bala equals their Ayana Bala).

4.6 Drik Bala / Drigbala (Aspect Strength)

Evaluates the angular distance θ = (λ_Aspecting - λ_Aspectee) mod 360°.
Aspect Value = piecewise linear function based on Parasara slokas:
- If 30° < θ <= 60°: Aspect = (θ - 30°) / 2
- If 60° < θ <= 90°: Aspect = (θ - 60°) + 15°
- If 90° < θ <= 120°: Aspect = (120° - θ) / 2 + 30°
- If 120° < θ <= 150°: Aspect = (150° - θ)
- If 150° < θ <= 180°: Aspect = (θ - 150°) × 2 (Special full aspect at 180° = 60 Virupas)
Special aspects added: Mars 4th/8th (100%), Jupiter 5th/9th (100%), Saturn 3rd/10th (100%).
Benefic aspects yield positive values; malefic aspects yield negative values.

6.2 Step 1: Trikona Shodhana (Trinal Reduction)

Signs are partitioned into 4 elemental triads (Trikonas):

  • Fire: Aries (1), Leo (5), Sagittarius (9)
  • Earth: Taurus (2), Virgo (6), Capricorn (10)
  • Air: Gemini (3), Libra (7), Aquarius (11)
  • Water: Cancer (4), Scorpio (8), Pisces (12)
For each triad {s₁, s₂, s₃} with bindus {b₁, b₂, b₃}:
min_val = min(b₁, b₂, b₃)
Reduced_Bindu(s) = b_s - min_val for each s in triad.
(If all three signs have equal bindus, all three become 0).

6.3 Step 2: Ekadhipatya Shodhana (Dual Ownership Reduction)

Applied to signs sharing a common planetary ruler (Mars: 1 & 8; Mercury: 3 & 6; Jupiter: 9 & 12; Venus: 2 & 7; Saturn: 10 & 11). Sun (5) and Moon (4) are single-owned and never reduced in this step:

Let the dual signs be s₁ and s₂ with post-Trikona bindus b₁ and b₂:
Case 1: Both signs have 0 bindus → No change.
Case 2: One sign has 0 bindus → The other sign retains its bindus.
Case 3: Both signs have planets occupied → Retain original bindus in both.
Case 4: Neither sign has a planet occupied:
If b₁ == b₂, set both to 0.
If b₁ != b₂, set both to min(b₁, b₂).
Case 5: One sign is occupied, one is unoccupied:
If occupied sign has fewer bindus than unoccupied sign, reduce unoccupied to occupied's count.
If occupied sign has more bindus, reduce unoccupied sign to 0.

6.4 Step 3: Pinda Sadhana (Rashi Pinda, Graha Pinda & Shodhya Pinda)

Rashi_Gunakara (Multipliers for signs 1 to 12):
[Aries: 7, Taurus: 10, Gemini: 8, Cancer: 4, Leo: 10, Virgo: 5, Libra: 7, Scorpio: 8, Sagittarius: 9, Capricorn: 5, Aquarius: 11, Pisces: 12]

[Sun: 5, Moon: 5, Mars: 8, Mercury: 5, Jupiter: 10, Venus: 7, Saturn: 5]
Graha_Pinda = Sum(Final_Reduced_Bindus[sign] × Graha_Gunakara[Planet_in_sign]) for all planets
