1.1 What is Ashtakavarga?

Ashtakavarga is derived from two Sanskrit roots: Ashta meaning "eight" and Varga meaning "division", "group", or "distribution". In Vedic Astrology, while a natal chart reveals planetary placements from the single vantage point of the Ascendant (Lagna), the Ashtakavarga system assesses the cumulative benefic and malefic influences cast upon each of the 12 zodiac signs simultaneously from eight distinct reference pivot points (Ashta Lagnas):

The 8 Reference Pivot Points (Ashta Lagnas): Parashara Axiom
ext{Reference Pivots} = { ext{Lagna, Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn}}
Rahu and Ketu, being lunar nodes (Chaya Grahas / Shadow Planets), do not possess independent bodies and hence cast no Ashtakavarga bindus in the classical Parashari system.

1.2 Bindu (Benefic Unit) vs. Rekha (Malefic Unit)

In classical Parashari treatises, each planet contributes a unit of benefic strength called a Bindu (dot or point, value $= 1$) in designated auspicious houses, while inauspicious houses receive a Rekha (line, or value $= 0$).

South Indian Convention (Tamil Nadu / Kerala)

• Bindu / Paral (பரல் / •): Auspicious point (Value $= 1$).
• Zero (0): Devoid of benefic point / Inauspicious.
• The cumulative total across all 7 planets and 12 signs is strictly 337 Bindus.
• Higher bindus ($ge 28$) signify strength, prosperity, and success.

North Indian Convention

• Rekha (|): Auspicious mark (Value $= 1$).
• Bindu (0): Inauspicious zero-point.
• Mathematically identical: both traditions evaluate the same 337 positive points in the Sarvashtakavarga table.

4.1 Financial Inflow vs. Outflow Balance (Varavinam vs. Selavinam)

Classical Thirukanitha and Parashari astrology employ an exact algebraic test to determine whether a native accumulates lifetime wealth or suffers persistent drain:

1. Total Inflow (Varavinam - வரவினம்):
ext{Varavinam} = ext{SAV}(H_2) + ext{SAV}(H_9) + ext{SAV}(H_{11})
• 2nd Bhava: Dhana Sthana (Accumulated Wealth)
• 9th Bhava: Bhagya Sthana (Fortune & Grace)
• 11th Bhava: Labha Sthana (Profits & Revenues)
2. Total Outflow (Selavinam - செலவினம்):
ext{Selavinam} = ext{SAV}(H_6) + ext{SAV}(H_8) + ext{SAV}(H_{12})
• 6th Bhava: Rina Sthana (Debts & Litigation)
• 8th Bhava: Randhra Sthana (Sudden Losses)
• 12th Bhava: Vyaya Sthana (Expenditures)
Net Financial Balance Equation: Delta = ext{Varavinam} - ext{Selavinam}

• Delta > 0 ( ext{Inflow} > ext{Outflow}): The native possesses high savings capability, easily clears obligations, acquires landed property, and enjoys lifelong financial independence.
• Delta < 0 ( ext{Outflow} > ext{Inflow}): Expenses persistently outstrip income; wealth drains rapidly; high susceptibility to borrowing or cash flow crises.
• Delta = 0: Income and expenditure remain in strict equilibrium.

5.1 The Purpose of Shodhana (Reduction Process)

The raw bindus tabulated in Bhinna Ashtakavarga represent potential energy. To determine the actual net functional power of each planet, Parashari astrology subjects the matrix to two successive mathematical reductions: Trikona Shodhana (Trinal Reduction) followed by Ekadhipatya Shodhana (Dual Lordship Reduction).

5.2 Trikona Shodhana (Trinal Reduction Rules)

Signs sharing the same triplicity (Trinal Group) are compared:
• Fire Triad: Aries, Leo, Sagittarius (1, 5, 9)
• Earth Triad: Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn (2, 6, 10)
• Air Triad: Gemini, Libra, Aquarius (3, 7, 11)
• Water Triad: Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces (4, 8, 12)

Trikona Shodhana Reduction Rules:
1. If the bindus in all three signs of a triad are equal, reduce all three to zero (0).
2. If the bindus are unequal, find the smallest value among the three signs and subtract this minimum value from all three signs (the minimum sign thus becomes 0).
3. If any sign in the triad already holds zero (0) bindus, no reduction is performed on that triad (values remain unchanged).

5.3 Ekadhipatya Shodhana (Dual Lordship Reduction Rules)

Applied exclusively to the five planets ruling dual signs: Mars (Aries/Scorpio), Mercury (Gemini/Virgo), Jupiter (Sagittarius/Pisces), Venus (Taurus/Libra), and Saturn (Capricorn/Aquarius). (The Sun and Moon rule only Leo and Cancer respectively and are exempt):

Planetary Occupation Scenario Ekadhipatya Reduction Rule
Both signs are occupied by planetsNo reduction is performed; bindus in both signs remain unchanged.
One sign is occupied, the other is vacantThe occupied sign retains all its bindus; the vacant sign's bindus are reduced to zero (0).
Both signs are vacant with equal bindusBoth signs are reduced to zero (0).
Both signs are vacant with unequal bindusThe smaller value is subtracted from the larger value; the smaller sign becomes zero (0).

5.4 Pinda Sadhana & Yoga Pinda Computation

Following both reductions, the resulting net bindus (Shodhita Bindus) are multiplied by specific canonical constants:

Rasi Gunakaras (Zodiac Multipliers)

Aries = 7, Taurus = 10, Gemini = 8, Cancer = 4, Leo = 10, Virgo = 5, Libra = 7, Scorpio = 8, Sagittarius = 9, Capricorn = 5, Aquarius = 11, Pisces = 12.
Rasi Pinda $= sum ( ext{Reduced Bindu} imes ext{Rasi Gunakara})$

Graha Gunakaras (Planetary Multipliers)

Sun = 5, Moon = 5, Mars = 8, Mercury = 5, Jupiter = 10, Venus = 7, Saturn = 5.
Graha Pinda $= sum ( ext{Reduced Bindu in Occupied Sign} imes ext{Graha Gunakara})$

Composite Yoga Pinda Equation:
ext{Yoga Pinda} = ext{Rasi Pinda} + ext{Graha Pinda}
The Yoga Pinda serves as the foundational parameter for classical Ayurdaya (longevity computation), Ashtama Shani timing, and identifying benefic transit trigger periods.
